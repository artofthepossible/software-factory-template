# Security Policy

## Supported versions

| Version | Supported |
|---------|-----------|
| latest  | Yes       |

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do **not** open a public issue. Report privately via
[GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/OWNER/REPO/security/advisories/new)
or email security@example.com.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 3 business days and provide a fix or mitigation plan within 30 days.
